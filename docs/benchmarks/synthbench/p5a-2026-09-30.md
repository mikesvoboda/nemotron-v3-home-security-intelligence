## What it says

- **The product model catches about a third of incidents at their level.** Qwen3-VL-8B:
  S3 36.5% [30.7–42.8] (n=241); 44.4% on threats and 2.2% on suspicious scenes. S2 6.7%
  [4.0–10.9] (n=209): no false alarm on benign scenes, 9.7% on hard negatives (hooded jogger 27.6%,
  power tools at night 17.2%).
- **The flagship catches more at a similar false-alarm rate.** S3 58.9% [52.6–65.0]: 69.4% on
  threats, 13.3% on suspicious scenes. S2 8.6% [5.5–13.2], 11.7% on hard negatives.
- **Both misses are mostly low scores, not rejections.** Both models confirm the declared objects
  on 96–98% of stills. 62.7% of Qwen's incident scores fall below the declared band, by 54 points
  on average. The shipped prompt verifies detections, and the risk score follows conservatively.
- **The two models agree on 79.1% of items.** On 72 items the flagship is right where Qwen is wrong;
  on 22 it is the reverse.
- **The declared truth held up.** The owner audited 60 stills: scene, people and conditions matched
  on all 60 (error 0.0% [0.0–6.0]); the prop was missing in 1 of 15 (6.7% [1.2–29.8]). No event
  was a generation error.
