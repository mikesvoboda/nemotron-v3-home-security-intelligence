## 3. The sweep, in one paragraph

S2 2.9% to 20.1% and S3 27.4% to 57.3% across the models against 10.0% and 39.0% for the shipped Qwen3-VL-8B Q4_K_M; no arm approaches the S3 bar (best Gemma 4 26B-A4B, 57.3%, at 20.1% false alarms); stranger-or-intent incidents score at most 7 of 64 in any arm. **The OD-26 selection rule was set on 2026-10-04 at the owner's words "set the selection rule and sweep report", after the readings were seen; its content is the agent's [A]**: the shipped 8B is the yardstick, a paired bootstrap that resamples whole scenarios (31), gates on refusals (at most 2) and peak VRAM (at most 18.4 GiB, an assumption pending the S1 re-take), no clear harm and a clear benefit; the output is a shortlist for holdout confirmation, never a pick. **No arm advances** (nearest miss: Qwen3.8-27B Q4_K_M, S3 +10.4 points, interval -0.5 to +24.0). ## 4. The owner's video question (unanswered in full)

"I think the current AI pipeline only extracts stills out of the videos. Can these models process video directly? Might they perform better if they could look at the video directly?" All six investigators' answers are in `docs/plans/2026-10-04-video-question-research.md` (unaudited, [A]). In short: the detector extracts stills from video for detection and deletes them; the VLM path extracts nothing and a clip fails as `verification_failed` (ISS-002); the shipped llama.cpp `b7972` is image-only and `b11376` accepts video only by ffmpeg frame extraction, with no ffmpeg in the `ai-vlm` image and about 20K tokens for a 10 s clip against a 16,384-token slot; the Qwen families are native-video models on the transformers/vLLM stack, Gemma 4 takes frames only; the literature says temporal input helps truly temporal tasks but gains on security tasks are modest and nothing tests home-camera risk scoring; in our corpus only about 18-19 of 241 incidents are time-extended by definition. **Could existing clips test it without new rendering? A small test, yes (3c, unaudited).** 164 ready H3 clips (144 benign, 17 incident, 3 ambiguous); all 17 incidents are stranger-or-intent scenes (loitering 5, tailgating 5, trying_car_doors 4, peering_into_windows 2, casing_with_phone 1); every clip is 1344x768, 24 fps, 243 frames (10.1 s) and was made image-to-video from a source still already in the sweep store, so its still score exists. On those 17 incident stills 13 of 15 arms score 0 of 17 and two score 1 of 17: the still baseline is a floor effect. Smallest design: clip frame 0, 4 and 8 evenly spaced frames as images, and native `video_url`, on the shipped 8B over 161 items (about 29 to 49 minutes, a computed estimate). A paired sign test on 17 clips needs at least 5 flips from 0 (p = 0.031), so only effects of about 30 points or more are detectable; the owner's 10-point rule in doc 18 is not testable at n = 17. Native video is not runnable as built (no ffmpeg in the `ai-vlm` image); 8 frames needs a transport shim. A null result would not show video cannot help (synthetic 10 s micro-motion clips, unaudited truth, one clip does not start from its source render); a positive one would not show real-camera video helps. **Needs the owner:** extracting frames from the existing mp4s creates derived images (the in-memory shim route is the safe reading of "do not render"), and frame bursts through the product VLM were rejected in the clips design (C1) and OD-5.

## 7. Facts learned the hard way

- **Push with the GitHub URL**, not `origin` (a host path that does not exist in the sandbox): `git push -u https://github.com/mikesvoboda/nemotron-v3-home-security-intelligence.git HEAD:refs/heads/<branch>`; the proxy injects credentials. Use `gh api -X PATCH repos/<repo>/pulls/<n> -F body=@file` to edit a PR body (`gh pr edit` trips on a deprecated GraphQL field). Commit with one-shot `-c user.name=... -c user.email=...`, never `git config --global`.
- **Prettier (3.2.4 via `npx --yes prettier@3.2.4 --write`, run twice) corrupts unbackticked identifiers with underscores and code spans closed with the wrong character**, and it reformats `*x*` to `_x_`. After formatting, compare content with whitespace and table padding stripped. The ledger is not a prettier fixed point at main: do not format it.
- **Verifiers find real errors.** About a third of the statements in each agent-built page were wrong, overclaimed or stale when an independent verifier re-read them. Do not skip verification on anything that carries numbers or citations.
- **Never `pkill -f` with a pattern that appears in your own command line** (it kills your shell), and never kill every `curl` for a host to stop one transfer (it killed the model downloader).
- The docs gate (`scripts/check-vss-docs-currency.py`) recomputes the register's counts from its `#### ISS-nnn` blocks; update the header count, Dashboard, area headings and P0/P1 list in the same commit as a block, and allocate ids with `python scripts/vss-next-id.py iss`. The README must not quote the three gated status phrases.
- **Main is already red** for reasons that predate this work: the frontend image scan (`pcre2`), the deploy smoke test, ZAP, the Linear 401s (the key is owner-held), AGENTS.md validation (the same 18 findings), and the nightly gate and analysis. Flaky Test Detection flaps.
