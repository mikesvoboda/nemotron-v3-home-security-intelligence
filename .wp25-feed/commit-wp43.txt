DECIDE: denominator = backend/services/ + backend/api/routes/ (the PLAN's
risk set); cadence stays weekly schedule + workflow_dispatch, never per-PR
(mutmut is hours at this scale). Score = mutmut's own badge,
(killed+timeout)/(total-skipped), verdict semantics IMPORTED from
mutmut.stats.status_by_exit_code and pinned in CI (test_mutation_score) so a
mutmut upgrade that moves exit-code meanings fails the pin instead of
silently moving the score. mutate_only_covered_lines=true scopes the score
to "tests catch behavior changes in code they execute"; the never-executed
complaint stays WP4.5's, and the every-run gap list keeps it visible.
